UI Storia is the design-system workbench for Symfony: it renders the real Twig templates and components of your project, documents their variants and tests them.

## Start here

- [Getting started](/docs/getting-started/): a component, three variants and a test in ten minutes.
- [Workflow](/docs/workflow/): how integrators and developers work on the same files.
- [Why not Storybook?](/docs/why-not-storybook/): what a port of Twig to JavaScript breaks.

## Reference

- [Installation](/docs/installation/) and [Interfaces](/docs/interfaces/): register the bundle and describe your views in YAML.
- [The interface](/docs/user-interface/), [Data](/docs/data/) and [Controls](/docs/controls/): browse the catalog, share data and edit arguments live.
- [Live reload](/docs/live-reload/), [Static export](/docs/export/) and [Testing](/docs/testing/): develop, publish and protect your catalog.
